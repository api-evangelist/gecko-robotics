# Gecko Robotics

> Gecko Robotics is a Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania AI and robotics company for critical
> physical infrastructure. Wall-climbing, aerial and submersible robots capture
> full-coverage ultrasonic, visual and other sensor data from boilers, pressure
> vessels, tanks, pipelines, ship hulls and decks. That data is fused with facility
> records and operational history inside Cantilever, the company's AI-powered
> operating platform for the built world, which quantifies corrosion rates, predicts
> remaining asset life and prioritizes maintenance and capital decisions across
> defense, power, oil and gas, steel, pulp and paper, mining and advanced
> manufacturing.

## API availability

Gecko Robotics publishes **no public API, developer portal, API reference, SDK,
CLI, MCP server, or A2A agent card**. Cantilever is delivered as a closed,
authenticated enterprise SaaS application at https://cantilever.app/ and access is
arranged through the company. As of 2026-07-31 an enrichment pass probed the
company website, the Cantilever application host, and the standard discovery paths
(`/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/graphql`, `/llms.txt`,
`/.well-known/*`) and found no machine-readable contract published for third-party
consumption. Integration inquiries go through the contact page.
